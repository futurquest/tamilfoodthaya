# UX & Branding Guidelines - Tamil Food Thaya

Our design philosophy bridge the gap between **Traditional Hospitality** and **Modern Efficiency**.

## 🤝 Trust & Reliability (Dutch UX)
1. **Directness**: Pricing and pickup times must be visible immediately.
2. **Accessibility**: High contrast text (Charcoal on Off-White) and responsive touch targets.
3. **Transparency**: Clear breakdown of BTW (VAT) and KvK information in the footer.
4. **Convenience**: iDEAL integrated directly via Stripe Checkout.

## 🍛 Warmth & Authenticity (Tamil Ethos)
1. **Color Therapy**: Use of `Maroon` to stimulate appetite and `Gold` to signify premium quality.
2. **Imagery**: High-resolution photography of authentic dishes like Kottu Roti and Dosa.
3. **Spice Levels**: Iconography (🌶️) to help customers navigate heat levels.
4. **Sticky Interaction**: Fixed WhatsApp button ensures customers can reach us instantly, reflecting traditional hospitality.

## 📱 Mobile Best Practices
- **Bottom Drawers**: The cart should slide from the bottom or side to be thumb-accessible.
- **Sticky CTAs**: "Bestel Nu" should be reachable without scrolling to the footer.
- **Micro-interactions**: Subtle hover states on menu cards and smooth page transitions via Framer Motion.
